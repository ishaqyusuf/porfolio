# Product

## What this is

Ishaq Yusuf's portfolio homepage: a dark product exhibit that proves senior full-stack product engineering through real work, not a skills list.

## Audience

Hiring managers and founders evaluating a remote senior product engineer, and businesses that need an operations platform built.

## Story, in order

1. **Headline:** "I build the software behind everyday business."
2. **GND Millwork (primary proof):** client operations platform, lead developer. A Sales → Payments → Dispatch walkthrough with illustrative sample data, plus the full module scope.
3. **afterservice (independent product):** follow-up software for local service businesses. Customer → Completed job → Follow-up → Outcome, with an illustrative contact log.
4. **Four open-source product labs:** SchoolClerk, PlotKeys, HalaalVest (under development) and EwaTrade (early stage).
5. **Capabilities** and undated **Experience** (roles listed by responsibility).
6. **About**, then the amber **contact** close (preview-only form, email, CV, GitHub, LinkedIn).

## Truth rules

- Claims come from the Codex dossier and the afterservice repo. No invented metrics, testimonials, customers, pricing or outcomes.
- Every product surface is labelled "Illustrative · sample data, not a product screenshot".
- afterservice is a private independent product: not open source, not warranty registration, not automatic messaging. AI and WhatsApp are "coming soon" only.
- Labs stay under development; EwaTrade stays early stage.
